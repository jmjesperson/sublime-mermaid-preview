MermaidPreview 0.2.0
====================

New setting: save_folder

Use it to choose the folder where the save dialog opens. Export PNG uses it.

    "save_folder": "~/Downloads"

- Use a full path. "~" means your home folder.
- The folder is used for every export, also when your document is saved in
  another folder. The file name still comes from your document.
- If the setting is empty, the dialog opens in the document's folder, as
  before.
- If the folder does not exist, the dialog opens in the document's folder,
  and the status bar shows a message.

To change it, open Preferences > Package Settings > MermaidPreview > Settings.
